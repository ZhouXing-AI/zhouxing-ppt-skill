# ECHO · 回响 — acoustic glass artwork

Single visual reference: [Overworld Audio](https://overworldaudio.com/). The presentation direction uses a calm midnight-blue atmosphere, mist-blue layers, pearl-white highlights and quiet typography. The SVGs are original acoustic abstractions rather than reproductions of the reference site's islands, markers, products or three-dimensional scene.

The material system is translucent acoustic glass: cool blue bodies, graphite-blue depth, fine pale edges, frosted layers and small warm-white interiors. It uses only native vector gradients and paths. There are no bitmap textures, remote resources, fonts, scripts, filters or heavy glow effects. Dark-blue contours keep the sculptures legible on a pale background; the deck may provide a gentle backing panel on dark slides.

Palette families: midnight and graphite blues around `#142D4F` and `#203B62`, mist and ice around `#91BEDD` and `#A4C9E0`, pearl `#EAF5F6`, and a restrained warm interior `#FFF3DD`. Most contours are 1.5–3 px at the native 1000 px viewBox. Curved silhouettes and overlapping optical planes establish the material; machined rails, green metal and source-site geography are deliberately absent.

There are 24 distinct sculptures, six in each semantic family:

- **listen / 聆听**: shell, lens, bowl, fan, droplets and shallow ripple.
- **tune / 调谐**: resonant vessel, harmonic fork, graduated fins, prisms, open dial and suspended reed.
- **connect / 联结**: paired waves, curved bridge, crossing ribbons, meeting arcs, braided waves and a shared cavity.
- **resonate / 回响**: memory loop, open horizon, nested chambers, overtone crown, stacked pulses and distant rings.

The six initial narrative anchors are `resonance-vessel`, `listening-shell`, `signal-lens`, `paired-waves`, `memory-loop` and `open-horizon`. All files use transparent 1000×1000 canvases. English/Chinese names, keywords and descriptions are included in `catalog.json`; original artwork is distributed under the accompanying MIT licence.
