SpaceGrotesk-Variable.ttf and DMMono-Regular.ttf are the original complete official bytes. BlendCJK.woff2 reuses the previously audited lossless complete Noto Sans SC WOFF2, preserving its full source cmap, glyph inventory, internal names, variable axes, outlines and metrics. It is not a content-specific subset. All three have fsType=0 and normal upright glyphs. Source/final file sizes, checksums, real axes, recommended weights, metadata and character ranges are recorded in font-manifest.json and *-coverage.json.

BlendSans / BlendMono / BlendCJK are CSS aliases only. Space Grotesk is variable 300–700: use real 400, 500 and 600. DM Mono Regular is real 400; do not synthesize bold or italic. Noto Sans SC is variable 100–900: use 500 titles and 400 body/UI. Preserve normal proportions and font-synthesis:none. Keep all OFL files, this notice and metadata when distributing the embedded HTML or Skill. CJK coverage remains bounded by the complete source repertoire, not all Unicode.

The original Noto TTF is retained in the development preparation workspace, not duplicated in this distributable font bundle. It is recoverable from the pinned source URL and source_ttf_sha256 in the manifest. The on-disk WOFF2 contains the full repertoire. No commercial DieGrotesk or ABCDiatype font is included.
