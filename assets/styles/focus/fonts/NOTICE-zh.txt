Complete official SC sources were losslessly compressed to WOFF2 using
fontTools 4.60.2 and Brotli 1.2.1 with preprocessing transforms disabled.

head.checkSumAdjustment and head.flags bit 11. FOCUS reuses the same
already audited WOFF2 bytes bundled in INDEX. No subsetting, outline edits,
metric edits or internal-name edits. These are complete SC-source fonts,
not the entire Unicode or pan-CJK repertoire.
